Keep your visual ideas together.

Organize images, videos, prompts, references, and ideas in a personal library on your iPhone or iPad.

Make room for inspiration

Save visual work and the prompts behind it. Add tags, keep references nearby, and return to an idea when you need it.

Your library works on your device without a separate account. Optional iCloud sync lets you use the same library across your Apple devices.

无需注册独立账户即可在设备上使用资料库；你也可以选择开启 iCloud，在自己的 Apple 设备间同步。
