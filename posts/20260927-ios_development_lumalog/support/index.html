技术支持 / Support

联系我们

使用图记时遇到问题，或有功能建议，请发送邮件至 theodoruszq@gmail.com。

为方便排查问题，你可以说明设备型号、iOS 或 iPadOS 版本、App 版本以及发生问题时的操作步骤。截图和资料库内容只需在你愿意分享时附上。

Get in touch

For app issues, general feedback, or feature requests, email theodoruszq@gmail.com.

To help diagnose an issue, you can include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and steps to reproduce it. Only attach screenshots or library content if you choose to share them.
