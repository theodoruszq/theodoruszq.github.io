DayBit 隐私政策

更新日期：2026-10-06

处理方式

DayBit 由 Theodorus 开发，用于每日文字和照片记录。App 不包含独立用户账号系统、广告或第三方分析 SDK，不向开发者服务器自动上传日记、照片或使用日志，也不将这些内容用于广告或追踪。记录内容由设备及你所选择的存储服务处理，用于保存、浏览、搜索、同步和导出日记。

记录与本地保存

你输入的文字、日期、照片、相关时间信息和偏好设置保存在设备上。App 会保存本地文字历史，并在部分资料库连接、迁移或恢复操作中保留备份，以减少意外覆盖造成的数据丢失。照片按导入设置压缩或保留原文件，可能包含拍摄时间等原有元数据；App 不保证去除全部照片元数据。当前版本不提供获取新位置的入口，也不在后台持续定位；旧记录或导入备份中的位置资料可能继续保留并包含在同步和导出中。

iCloud 云盘与自选文件夹

当系统允许本 App 使用 iCloud 云盘且服务可用时，DayBit 默认连接 iCloud 云盘中的 DayBit 文件夹，将文字、照片及相关记录元数据保存和同步到你的 Apple 账户，供自己的 Mac 和 iPhone 使用，并占用 iCloud 存储空间。iCloud 不可用时，记录先保存在本机。你可以在 App 设置中选择本地文件夹，并在系统设置中管理或关闭 DayBit 的 iCloud 使用；切换位置或关闭 iCloud 不会自动删除已有云端或旧文件夹中的副本。你选择的其他云盘或共享文件夹可能按该服务的设置同步或向他人提供访问。Apple 和你选择的存储服务按照各自的条款及隐私政策处理数据，开发者不通过自有服务器接收资料库。

照片、文件和通知权限

App 通过系统选择器读取你主动选择的照片和文件；你主动粘贴、拖放或导入的内容也会成为记录的一部分。保存照片到系统相册时，App 请求新增照片权限。只有你开启每日提醒时，App 才请求通知权限，并在设备上安排本地提醒，不向开发者发送日记内容。你可以在 App 中关闭提醒，或在系统设置中撤销相关权限；拒绝可选权限不影响文字记录。

导出与分享

TXT 导出包含日记文字、日期及相关说明，不包含照片文件；完整 .daybit 备份包含文字、照片和相关元数据。导出文件不由 App 额外加密。只有你主动导出、复制、保存照片或分享时，相关内容才交给你选择的位置、剪贴板或应用。请妥善保管导出文件；第三方应用和存储服务按其政策处理你交给它们的资料。

保留、删除与控制

日记和照片保留在你的设备及所选资料库中，直到你管理或删除这些文件；文字历史和备份没有按固定期限自动清理的承诺。移除记录中的照片不会自动删除图库中所有图片文件，编辑正文也不会自动删除旧文字历史。切换存储位置、关闭同步或卸载 App 不会删除所有云端、自选文件夹、系统备份和导出副本。需要彻底删除时，请先关闭相关设备上的 App，再分别清理本机 App 数据、iCloud 云盘的 DayBit 文件夹、自选资料库、历史与备份，以及外部副本，避免仍在同步的设备重新写入数据。开发者没有访问或远程删除你资料库的能力，可通过支持邮箱协助你找到并管理这些副本。

联系与支持

你主动发送至 theodoruszq@gmail.com 的邮箱地址、反馈文字及附件仅用于回复、排查问题和相关支持沟通，由邮箱服务商按其政策处理。App 不会自动发送反馈或附加日记。支持邮件保留用于后续沟通，直到不再需要或你请求删除；你可通过此邮箱请求删除支持邮件中提供的个人信息。

政策更新

本政策适用于 DayBit 的 Mac 与 iPhone 版本。数据处理方式变更时，我们会更新此页面及更新日期。隐私问题或数据管理请求请联系 theodoruszq@gmail.com。

DayBit Privacy Policy

Updated: October 6, 2026

Processing

DayBit is developed by Theodorus for daily text and photo journaling. It has no separate user account system, advertising, or third-party analytics SDK. It does not automatically upload journals, photos, or usage logs to developer-operated servers, or use them for advertising or tracking. Your device and chosen storage services process your content to save, browse, search, sync, and export your journal.

Records and local storage

Your text, dates, photos, associated timestamps, and preferences are stored on your device. The app keeps local text history and backups during some library connection, migration, or restore operations to reduce accidental data loss. Photos are compressed or kept as original files according to your import setting, and may retain capture dates and other metadata. The app does not guarantee removal of all photo metadata. The current version has no interface for capturing new locations and does not continuously track location in the background. Location data in older entries or imported backups may remain and be included in sync and exports.

iCloud Drive and custom folders

When system settings permit iCloud Drive access and the service is available, DayBit connects by default to the DayBit folder in iCloud Drive. Text, photos, and related record metadata are stored and synced through your Apple Account for use on your own Mac and iPhone, and count toward your iCloud storage. When iCloud is unavailable, records stay on your device. You may choose a local folder in app settings and manage or disable DayBit’s iCloud access in system settings. Changing locations or disabling iCloud does not automatically delete existing cloud or previous folder copies. Other cloud or shared folders you select may sync or allow access by others according to their settings. Apple and your chosen storage services handle data under their own terms and privacy policies. The developer does not receive your library through developer-operated servers.

Photos, files, and notification permissions

System pickers provide access to the photos and files you choose. Content you actively paste, drop, or import also becomes part of your records. Saving a photo to the system photo library requests add-only permission. Notification permission is requested when you enable daily reminders. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device and do not send journal content to the developer. Disable reminders in the app or revoke permissions in system settings. Declining optional permissions does not prevent text journaling.

Export and sharing

TXT exports contain journal text, dates, and related descriptions, but no photo files. Complete .daybit backups include text, photos, and related metadata. Exports are not additionally encrypted by the app. Content is passed to your chosen location, clipboard, or app only when you choose to export, copy, save a photo, or share. Store exports securely. Third-party apps and storage services process information you give them under their own policies.

Retention, deletion, and control

Journals and photos remain on your device and in the selected library until you manage or delete those files. There is no promised automatic expiry for text history or backups. Removing a photo from an entry does not automatically delete all image files from the media store; editing text does not automatically delete its history. Changing locations, disabling sync, or uninstalling the app does not delete all cloud, custom folder, system backup, or exported copies. For complete removal, close the app on connected devices, then separately remove local app data, the DayBit folder in iCloud Drive, custom libraries, history, backups, and external copies. Avoid letting devices that are still syncing write the data back. The developer cannot access or remotely delete your library, but can help you locate and manage copies through the support email.

Contact and support

Your email address, feedback, and attachments you choose to send to theodoruszq@gmail.com are used only to respond, investigate issues, and provide related support. Email service providers process them under their policies. The app does not automatically send feedback or attach journals. Support correspondence is retained for follow-up until it is no longer needed or you request deletion. Email this address to request deletion of personal information provided in support correspondence.

Policy updates

This policy applies to the Mac and iPhone versions of DayBit. We will update this page and its date when data handling changes. For privacy questions or data management requests, contact theodoruszq@gmail.com.
