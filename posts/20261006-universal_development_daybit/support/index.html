技术支持 / Support

联系我们

使用 DayBit 时遇到问题，或有功能建议，请邮件联系 theodoruszq@gmail.com。你可以提供设备型号、macOS 或 iOS 版本、App 版本以及复现步骤。截图或日记内容仅在你愿意分享时附上，请先移除敏感信息。

记录没有保存？

文字会自动保存。如果出现保存失败提示，请先保留当前窗口中的文字，检查设备可用空间并重试。Mac 上也可以按 ⌘S 立即保存。

Mac 和 iPhone 的记录没有同步？

请确认两台设备使用同一 Apple 账户，已开启 iCloud 云盘，并在 DayBit 设置中选择“iCloud 云盘”。检查网络和 iCloud 可用空间，等待系统完成文件上传和下载。iCloud 不可用时，记录先保存在本机；选择本地文件夹时，该位置的跨设备同步由你使用的存储服务决定。

如何备份与恢复？

在设置的导出与备份区域选择“导出完整备份 (.daybit)”，将文件保存到可信位置；在另一台设备选择“从备份恢复…”导入。TXT 导出便于阅读，但不包含照片文件。同步不能替代独立备份，建议定期保存完整备份。

每天提醒没有出现？

请在 App 设置中开启“每天提醒”并选择时间，同时在系统通知设置中允许 DayBit 发送通知。当天已经写过就不再提醒；专注模式和系统通知设置也可能影响提醒显示。

如何管理或删除数据？

记录、照片及历史副本保存在设备和所选资料库位置。移除日记中的照片不会自动清理所有已保存的图片副本，切换存储位置也不会删除旧资料库。如需彻底删除，请关闭相关设备上的 DayBit，并分别处理本机 App 数据、iCloud 云盘中的 DayBit 文件夹、自选资料库、历史及备份文件；不要仅删除其中一端后继续同步。需要帮助时请联系支持邮箱。

Get in touch

For issues or feature requests, email theodoruszq@gmail.com. Include your device model, macOS or iOS version, app version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Attach screenshots or journal content only if you choose to share them, and remove sensitive information first.

My writing has not saved

Text saves automatically. If a save error appears, keep a copy of the text in the current window, check available device storage, and retry. On Mac, press ⌘S to save immediately.

My Mac and iPhone are not syncing

Use the same Apple Account on both devices, enable iCloud Drive, and select iCloud Drive in DayBit settings. Check your network and available iCloud storage, then allow time for uploads and downloads. When iCloud is unavailable, records stay on the device. Synchronization of a custom folder depends on the storage service you use.

How do I back up and restore my journal?

Choose Export Complete Backup (.daybit) in settings and save it somewhere you trust. Use Restore from Backup on another device to import it. TXT exports are readable text and do not include photo files. Sync does not replace an independent backup; keep complete backups regularly.

Daily reminders are missing

Enable the daily reminder and choose a time in the app, then allow DayBit notifications in system settings. A reminder is skipped if you have already written that day. Focus modes and system notification settings can also affect delivery.

How do I manage or delete data?

Records, photos, and historical copies are stored on your device and in your selected library location. Removing a photo from an entry does not automatically remove every stored image copy. Changing storage locations does not delete the previous library. For complete removal, close DayBit on connected devices and separately remove local app data, the DayBit folder in iCloud Drive, custom libraries, history, and backup files. Avoid deleting only one side while sync remains active. Contact support if you need help.
