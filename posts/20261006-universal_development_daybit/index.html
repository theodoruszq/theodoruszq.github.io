DayBit

一点一滴，都是日常。
Every day, a little story.

记下今天，留给以后的自己

DayBit 是一个轻巧的每日记录应用。打开就开始写，用几句话、一张照片，留下今天的想法和生活片段。

按自己的节奏记录

文字自动保存，可以添加照片，也可以通过日历回到过去的某一天补记。每天提醒由你选择开启，帮你慢慢养成记录习惯。

回顾日常

在时间线中按年月浏览记录，通过文字或日期搜索，找回那些值得记住的小事。调整字体、字号和间距，让书写更舒适。

带着记录继续走

通过 iCloud 云盘在自己的 Mac 和 iPhone 之间同步，也可以选择本地文件夹保存。导出 TXT 阅读文字，或导出包含文字和照片的完整 .daybit 备份。无需注册独立账户。

Write today. Revisit it someday.

DayBit is a simple daily journal. Open it and start writing. A few words or a photo can capture your thoughts and the little moments of everyday life.

Write at your own pace

Your writing saves automatically. Add photos or use the calendar to revisit and write about a previous day. Enable a daily reminder when you want a gentle nudge to keep the habit.

Look back on everyday moments

Browse your journal by year and month in the timeline, or search by text or date. Choose your font, text size, and spacing for a comfortable writing experience.

Keep your journal with you

Use iCloud Drive to sync between your own Mac and iPhone, or choose a local folder. Export text as TXT, or create a complete .daybit backup with text and photos. No separate account registration is required.
