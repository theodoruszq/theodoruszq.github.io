它做什么What it does

CoolCaption 捕获 Mac 的系统输出音频，使用 Apple 本地语音模型转写，并通过 Apple Translation 翻译。适合观看外语视频、收听音频和练习听力。CoolCaption captures your Mac’s system audio, transcribes it with Apple’s on-device speech models and translates through Apple Translation. Great for foreign-language video, audio and listening practice.

系统要求Requirements

macOS 26 或更高版本，且当前 Mac 支持 Apple SpeechTranscriber。首次使用需授予“屏幕与系统音频录制”和“语音识别”权限，并下载语言资源。macOS 26 or later and a Mac that supports Apple SpeechTranscriber. First use requires Screen & System Audio Recording and Speech Recognition permissions plus language downloads.

请注意Good to know

可用的识别与翻译语言取决于系统资源；英语识别通常最稳定。转写及翻译结果可能有误，请核对重要内容。Available recognition and translation languages depend on system resources; English is usually the most reliable. Transcription and translation may contain errors—verify what matters.
