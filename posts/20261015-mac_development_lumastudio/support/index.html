Getting started

Import a photo or folder using the + button. Double-click a photo to open the editor. Pick a Profile to begin, then refine the controls in the Edit panel. Compare with the original before exporting.

RAW files and requirements

LumaStudio requires macOS 26 or later. It supports high-resolution 6K+ RAW processing, subject to the camera RAW formats supported by your macOS version. JPEG, HEIC, PNG and TIFF can also be imported. If a RAW file will not open, include your camera model and file extension in your support request.

Keep editing after the model

The model creates editable adjustments. Change the light and color controls to refine the result. You do not need a fixed .CUBE file to get started.

Saving and exporting

Edits are saved locally in your LumaStudio library. Use Export to create a JPEG or PNG in a folder you choose. The imported original is preserved; exported images are separate files.

Where are my photos?

LumaStudio keeps its own local library. Importing a photo does not replace its source file. Removing an item from the app and deleting an exported file are separate actions.

Purchase questions

LumaStudio is planned as a one-time paid Mac App Store download. Pricing in your region is shown by the App Store. Billing and refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Report a problem

Email theodoruszq@gmail.com with your macOS version, LumaStudio version, camera/file type, and the steps that caused the issue. Screenshots can help; remove private information before sending. Do not send private photos unless you choose to share them for troubleshooting.
