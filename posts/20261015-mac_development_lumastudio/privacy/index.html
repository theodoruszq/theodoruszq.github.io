Privacy policy

LumaStudio processes your photos locally on your Mac.

Information collected by the app

LumaStudio does not collect or transmit your photos, photo metadata, editing history, usage analytics or advertising identifiers to the developer. It contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not require an account.

Photos and local storage

When you import photos, LumaStudio stores copies of the originals, previews, edit recipes, photo metadata and locally generated search keywords in its local app library. Settings, presets, albums and permissions for export folders are stored locally. This information supports editing, browsing, search and export.

On-device processing

The bundled Luma Tone model and Apple Vision features run on your device. Photos are not sent to a cloud model for editing or classification.

Permissions

LumaStudio accesses files or folders you select through macOS file dialogs. If you choose to save to Apple Photos, macOS may request permission to add the exported photo to your library. You can manage app permissions in System Settings.

Retention and deletion

Local library data remains on your Mac until you remove it. Deleting a photo from LumaStudio removes the app’s stored session and imported copy; it does not delete a separately stored source file. Deleting an exported file is handled separately in Finder or Photos. Contact support if you need help removing the entire local library.

Purchases and Apple services

The Mac App Store handles purchases, billing and download history under Apple’s privacy policy. Apple may also process diagnostics if you choose to share them through system settings. LumaStudio does not operate its own analytics or payment server.

Support correspondence

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include, and uses it to respond and troubleshoot. Do not include private photos or sensitive information unless needed for your request. You can ask for support correspondence to be deleted.

This website

These product, privacy and support pages are static files hosted by GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics scripts or advertising trackers. GitHub may process standard request and server information such as IP addresses under its own privacy policy.

Children and changes

LumaStudio is a general-purpose photo editor and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information. This policy may change when app functionality changes; the updated date appears above.
